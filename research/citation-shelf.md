# Citation shelf — verified authorities for the reflection

> The reusable list of psychologists, theorists, studies, and thinkers the channel
> can cite in a reflection beat. **Verified once here, cited many times** — so we
> don't re-verify Woolley or Dweck every episode, and so no claim goes out in the
> narrator's own voice with no owner (the T07 failure, `research/dieck-docs/structure-analysis.md`).
>
> **How it's used:** `brain/09` §A6 + §"Ensayo" — every load-bearing psychological
> reading beat names an authority from this shelf and carries `[S..]`. On the
> **Ensayo** track this runs through the whole episode, not just the close
> (`brain/20` §3). Stage-5 fact-check (`brain/14`) crosses every cited authority in
> `05-script.md` against this file.
>
> **Adding one:** an episode that needs a new authority adds the row here *and*
> reads the primary source (or a Tier-A/B secondary that quotes it directly)
> before the script passes fact-check. `Verified` flips to ✅ only when someone has
> actually checked the wording of the claim against the source. Field matters —
> attribute a pediatrician as a pediatrician, a story theorist as a story theorist,
> never all as "psychologists".
>
> **Not a substitute for `03-source-log.csv`.** Each cited row still gets an
> `[S..]` in the episode's own source-log with the specific edition/page/URL used.

## Legend

- **Field** — how the person is correctly attributed on screen.
- **Claim we cite it for** — the specific idea, in our words. If the episode needs
  a *different* claim from the same author, add a new row; don't stretch this one.
- **Primary source** — the book/paper/talk where they say it. Tier A (their own
  work) or Tier B (scholarship quoting them directly).
- **Verified** — ✅ someone checked the exact wording · ⏳ on the shelf, not yet
  checked · ⚠️ checked and the popular version overstates it (cite carefully).
