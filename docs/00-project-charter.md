# 00 — Project Charter

## Mission

Produce Spanish-language journalistic documentary episodes that (1) narrate a verifiable real case, (2) reflect on what it reveals about human psychology and behavior, and (3) connect — surgically, at the end, never forced — to an applicable lesson for entrepreneurs, dreamers, workers, people of faith, immigrants, and similar audiences.

**Exodo Channel is a standalone YouTube channel** run by Carmen and Josh. It is not a phase of, funnel for, or sub-brand of any other venture. It succeeds or fails on its own terms: audience that returns because the work is rigorous and worth their time. **Source rigor and credibility matter as much as entertainment value.**

## Team

| Person | Role |
|--------|------|
| Carmen | Editorial lead, research direction, ideation of track **T01 Historias Inspiradoras**, fact-check sign-off (Layer 3). Journalist, ex-university lecturer. Narrates / on camera in a share of episodes. |
| Josh | **Writes every script.** Ideation of track **T02 Exploración**, production, edit, publishing, tech, `tools/`. Narrates / on camera in a share of episodes. |

**Two content tracks**, one owner each — see [ideas/tracks.md](../ideas/tracks.md). **Josh writes all scripts;** the "checker ≠ writer" principle is preserved by two automated fact-check passes plus **Carmen's** sign-off ([14-fact-check-protocol.md](14-fact-check-protocol.md)). **Narration is per-episode** (Carmen or Josh), recorded in `episodes/_STATUS.md`.

## Audience

Broad Spanish-speaking audience — Monterrey to Madrid to Buenos Aires. Not tied to any city or local scene.

## What this project IS

- Episodes narrating historical cases, public figures, companies, or practices **that existed** — always with verifiable, public sources.
- Each episode combines: journalistic narrative + psychological/human reflection + practical applicable takeaway, linked surgically at the end.
- Spanish-language content for a wide audience.

## What this project IS NOT — non-negotiable constraints

1. **Subjects are public.** Every episode is about a **public figure, a historical case, or a company/practice with verifiable public documentation** — not private individuals, not cases without a public record. This is the channel's value proposition, not an administrative rule.
2. **No episode subject drawn from people Carmen or Josh personally know** — not even anonymized. Privacy, consent, and conflict of interest. The founders' own migration story is *voice and motive* (About page, the reflection), never an episode subject.
3. **No factual claim without a cited source.** No invented data or quotes, ever.
4. The channel stays **independent in brand, folder, and workflow** — no co-branding, cross-posting, or shared identity with any other channel or venture.

## Success criteria

- Consistent publishing rhythm sustained without burning the non-negotiables.
- Every published episode survives an external fact-check with zero uncited claims.
- Returning audience — retention and subscribers who come back for the next one, measured, not assumed.

## Change control

This charter and the other `docs/` files are **standing rules**. Changing a non-negotiable requires explicit sign-off from both Carmen and Josh, recorded in the git history of this file.
