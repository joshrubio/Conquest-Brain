# 08 — Tone of Voice

> **Status: v0 — working hypothesis.** Written from the general Dieck Docs register + service-journalism norms, not from a transcript study. Refine against `research/dieck-docs/` findings, then bump the version.

Applies to every script regardless of narrator. Two narrators (Carmen, Josh) — the **channel voice is one**; personal delivery differs, the writing standard does not.

## 1. Register

- **Precise, calm, adult.** The narrator knows the story cold and is not impressed by their own material.
- **Curiosity over verdict.** We investigate, we don't prosecute. The audience reaches the judgment; we lay out the record.
- **Respect for the subject**, including subjects who did wrong. No contempt, no mockery, no gloating at a downfall.
- **No moralizing.** State what happened and what it suggests; don't lecture.
- **Restraint in the payoff.** The insight lands harder when the delivery is quiet.

## 2. Language

- **Spanish neutral-international.** No Rotterdam / Netherlands in-jokes, no strong regionalisms, no slang that dates fast. Understandable from Monterrey to Madrid to Buenos Aires.
- Concrete nouns and verbs. Cut adjectives that only add heat ("escalofriante", "brutal", "increíble").
- Numbers said in a way the ear can hold ("casi ocho veces su valor", not "un 782%").
- Foreign terms and names: pronounce consistently; gloss on first use if needed.
- Translated quotes: always flagged ("en sus palabras, traducidas:").

## 3. Sentence rhythm (for narration)

- Mostly short-to-medium sentences. One idea per sentence in tense passages.
- Vary length deliberately: a run of short sentences to build pressure, one longer sentence to resolve.
- Read every script aloud before lock. If the narrator stumbles, rewrite the sentence, don't just re-take.

## 4. Attribution phrasing (use liberally)

"según los registros…", "en su autobiografía…", "la investigación parlamentaria concluyó…", "las fuentes discrepan sobre…", "no hay registro de…", "esto no se sabe con certeza…".

## 5. What we never do

- Clickbait the intro with a promise the body doesn't pay off.
- Invent a subject's inner thoughts and state them as fact ("pensó que…"). Use "es posible que pensara…" or cut.
- Sensationalize violence, death, illness, or abuse. Sober, non-graphic, help-resource note where relevant.
- Reproduce song lyrics; quote long verbatim passages of copyrighted text.
- Pitch the membership business. Soft channel CTA only.
- Use contempt-by-editing: music stings, sarcastic pauses, "and then, of course…".

## 6. Direct address

- Rare in the narrative and reflection ("tú"/"vos" mostly absent there).
- Allowed in the final applied takeaway only, and gently: "si estás construyendo algo…", "cuando te toque decidir con prisa…".
