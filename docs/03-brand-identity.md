# 03 — Brand Identity

**Name: Éxodo** (wordmark / handles: **"Exodo Official"**, `@exodoofficial` — plain `@exodo` / `@canal_exodo` are taken). Chosen 2026-08-28. The full trail of ~30 options considered is in [brand/naming-exploration.md](../brand/naming-exploration.md).

## Name — rationale & open items

- **Meaning:** *éxodo* = a mass departure. For Carmen and Josh it is literal — they are **Venezuelan journalists**, part of the ~7.7M-person Venezuelan *éxodo*. The name carries the founder story and the channel's thesis: the difference between the official version and what actually happened, told by people who left because the official version was a lie.
- **Spelling:** prose / subtitles use **"Éxodo"** (correct Spanish). Wordmark and handles use **"Exodo"** (no diacritic — handles don't take accents; cleaner internationally). Confirm this split is what we want.
- **Accepted trade-offs** (see [brand/naming-exploration.md](../brand/naming-exploration.md) for the full analysis):
  - The name leans thematic (migration / exile). Part of the audience will read it as a migration channel even though episodes are broad. Decision: lean into it — the founders' story is the brand.
  - "Éxodo / Exodus" is a crowded term: *Exodus* crypto wallet (different trademark class — 36, financial), the band, the Marley album, biblical documentaries, and Spanish-journalism usage around Venezuelan migration (Radio Ambulante, Revista 5W). No Spanish-language documentary **channel** owns it — that lane is open.
  - Discovery via name-search will be noisy. Accepted (channel discovery on YouTube is driven by thumbnails/titles/suggested, not name-search).
- **Open items (do before investing in assets):**
  - [ ] Trademark search in **class 41** (education/entertainment) for "Éxodo" / "Exodo".
  - [ ] Secure handles: `@exodoofficial` on YouTube, IG, TikTok — same across platforms.
  - [ ] Domain: `exodo.com` / `exodo.tv` almost certainly taken → `exodooficial.*` or a modifier.

## Hard constraints on the brand

1. **Separate from the membership business.** Different logo, handles, visual language. A viewer must not read this channel as the business's channel. No shared wordmark, no "by [business]" tag on-screen.
2. **Separate from any other team video channel.** No shared branding, playlists, or cross-posting that merges identities.
3. Identity must work for a **broad Spanish-speaking audience** — not tied to Rotterdam, the Netherlands, or a local scene.
4. Must not imply the channel covers private individuals or local community stories. It signals: history, real cases, evidence, human insight.
   - **Sharper because of the name:** "Éxodo" must never be used to tell a *Rotterdam-community* migration story. The channel does public, historical, documented cases only. The Venezuelan/founder angle lives in the About text and the reflection, not in episode subjects sourced from the community.

## Decisions taken

- **Name:** Éxodo / "Exodo Official". 2026-08-28.
- **Brand type:** independent show brand — no real person's name (fits Carmen + Josh both narrating; scales).
- **Name tone:** the name is thematic/evocative rather than the "rigorous-archival" register explored earlier; the *content* still holds the rigor.
- **Founder story:** front and centre — Carmen and Josh are Venezuelan journalists; the *éxodo* is why "verified vs. official version" matters to them. Goes in the channel About / a pinned intro video.

## Channel-type decision (open)

Undecided: **own new YouTube channel** vs. **recurring segment inside an existing team channel**.

- If **own channel:** *Éxodo* is the channel name + handle.
- If **segment:** *Éxodo* is a sub-brand — series name, dedicated playlist, consistent title prefix, distinct thumbnail system — inside a host channel whose identity does **not** belong to the membership business.
- Blueprint works either way. Lock this before the first publish.
