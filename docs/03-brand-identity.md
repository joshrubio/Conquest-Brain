# 03 — Brand Identity

**Name: Cross Check.** Chosen 2026-08-28. Differentiator when a plain handle is unavailable: **"Official"** (e.g. `@crosscheckofficial`, "Cross Check Official"). The full trail of options considered is in [brand/naming-exploration.md](../brand/naming-exploration.md).

## Name — rationale & open items

- **Meaning:** "cross-check" = to verify a claim against independent sources. It *is* the channel's method ([01-editorial-and-sourcing.md](01-editorial-and-sourcing.md)).
- **Style:** two short words, punchy cadence, English (fine for a Spanish-language channel — cf. *Dieck Docs*).
- **Known collisions (accepted):** *CrossCheck Media* (US TV production company), *CrossCheck, Inc.* (US payments, since 1983), *CrossCheck* (First Draft/Google journalism-verification project, 2017, now dormant). None operate as a Spanish-language documentary YouTube channel — that lane is open.
- **Open items (do before investing in assets):**
  - [ ] Trademark search in **class 41** (education/entertainment) — *CrossCheck Media* is the one to clear.
  - [ ] Secure handles: YouTube, IG, TikTok. Plain `@crosscheck` likely taken → fall back to `@crosscheckofficial` (or `…docs` / `…tv`). Same name across platforms.
  - [ ] Domain: `crosscheck.com` almost certainly taken → `.tv` / `.co` / a modifier.

## Hard constraints on the brand

1. **Separate from the membership business.** Different logo, handles, visual language. A viewer must not read this channel as the business's channel. No shared wordmark, no "by [business]" tag on-screen.
2. **Separate from any other team video channel.** No shared branding, playlists, or cross-posting that merges identities.
3. Identity must work for a **broad Spanish-speaking audience** — not tied to Rotterdam, the Netherlands, or a local scene.
4. Must not imply the channel covers private individuals or local community stories. It signals: history, real cases, evidence, human insight.

## Decisions taken

- **Name:** Cross Check (+ "Official" as handle differentiator). 2026-08-28.
- **Brand type:** independent show brand — no real person's name (fits Carmen + Josh both narrating; scales).
- **Name tone:** rigorous / archival register — evidence, verification.
- **Founder story (for the manifesto, not the name):** Carmen and Josh are Venezuelan journalists. The Venezuelan *éxodo* is part of who they are and why the "verified vs. official version" thesis matters to them — this belongs in the About text / a pinned intro, not in the channel name.

## Channel-type decision (open)

Undecided: **own new YouTube channel** vs. **recurring segment inside an existing team channel**.

- If **own channel:** *Cross Check* is the channel name + handle.
- If **segment:** *Cross Check* is a sub-brand — series name, dedicated playlist, consistent title prefix, distinct thumbnail system — inside a host channel whose identity does **not** belong to the membership business.
- Blueprint works either way. Lock this before the first publish.

## Visual direction (to define)

- Narrator framing standard (Carmen or Josh on camera): shot size, background, lighting note.
- Archival treatment: framing, grain/vignette, caption style for documents.
- Lower-third / source-citation caption style — leans into the "cross-check" idea (a verification stamp, a checked box, a redaction bar).
- Typography: one display + one text face.
- Color: restrained palette; must differ from the membership business palette.
- Thumbnail system: face vs. archival, text length cap, contrast rules.
