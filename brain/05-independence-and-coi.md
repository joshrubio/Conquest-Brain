# 05 — Independence & Conflict of Interest

Keeps the founders' other roles and private lives out of the episodes. Checked at two gates: idea intake (rubric) and fact-check (Layer 3). Any breach → pull the episode.

*(Brand/identity independence — own channel, no co-branding, no third-party pitch — lives in [03-brand-identity.md](03-brand-identity.md).)*

## 1. Off-limits as an episode subject — always

- **Anyone Usuario 001 or Usuario 002 personally know** — friends, family, colleagues, neighbours, community contacts — named, described, or identifiable, **even anonymized**.
- **Any story that reached the team privately** — a message, a tip, an off-record conversation — rather than through the public record.
- **The founders' own migration story** as a subject. It is *voice and motive* (channel About, the reflection), never the case on the table.

## 2. The public-documentation test

> *Would this story be tellable entirely from public documentation by a journalist who has never met anyone involved?*

If **no** → it is not an Exodo episode. This is the operative COI gate; the rubric's E4/E5 and the fact-check sheet both apply it.

## 3. If an idea surfaces from private life

It may be pursued **only if** a genuine *public* version exists (a public figure, a historical case, a documented company) — and then it is **re-sourced from scratch** from public material, with the reset noted in `02-research-dossier.md`.

## 4. Research hygiene

- Public sources only ([01-editorial-and-sourcing.md](01-editorial-and-sourcing.md)). No private records, CRMs, or personal correspondence as source material.
- The idea pool (`ideas/idea-pool.md`) is generated from public material and imports from nowhere else.
- No third-party collaborator with repo access without a pass through this file — scripts name real people before publication.
