# 00 — Project Charter

## Mission

Produce Spanish-language journalistic documentary episodes that (1) narrate a verifiable real case, (2) reflect on what it reveals about human psychology and behavior, and (3) connect — surgically, at the end, never forced — to an applicable lesson for entrepreneurs, dreamers, workers, people of faith, immigrants, and similar audiences.

**Conquest-Oficial is a standalone YouTube channel** run by Usuario 001 and Usuario 002. It is not a phase of, funnel for, or sub-brand of any other venture. It succeeds or fails on its own terms: audience that returns because the work is rigorous and worth their time. **Source rigor and credibility matter as much as entertainment value.**

## Team

| Slot | Responsibilities (this iteration's assignment) |
|------|------|
| Usuario 001 | **Writes every script.** Production, edit, publishing, tech, `tools/`. Ideation (shared — both tracks). Narrates / on camera in a share of episodes. |
| Usuario 002 | Editorial lead, research direction. Ideation (shared — both tracks). Narrates / on camera in a share of episodes. |

Roles are independent and responsibilities are assignable items — the split above is the current assignment, not a fixed identity. Real people behind each slot: see [USERS.md](USERS.md).

**Two format tracks — Documental and Ensayo** — defined by format and rights regime, not theme; ideation is shared (either founder proposes to either track). See [ideas/tracks.md](../ideas/tracks.md). **Usuario 001 writes all scripts.** Fact-check is fully automated — L1 deterministic + an L2 agent edit-pass that applies the corrections ([14-fact-check-protocol.md](14-fact-check-protocol.md)). No human step there. Every other gate can be signed by one person. **Narration is per-episode**, recorded in `episodes/_STATUS.md`.

## Audience

Broad Spanish-speaking audience — Monterrey to Madrid to Buenos Aires. Not tied to any city or local scene.

## What this project IS

- Episodes narrating historical cases, public figures, companies, practices, or documented collective phenomena **that existed** — always with verifiable, public sources. For the **Ensayo** track, also a publicly released creative work and its documented public reception, treated as criticism / commentary / cultural history ([20-experimental-clip-protocol.md](20-experimental-clip-protocol.md)).
- Each episode combines: journalistic narrative + psychological/human reflection + practical applicable takeaway, linked surgically at the end.
- Spanish-language content for a wide audience.

## What this project IS NOT — non-negotiable constraints

1. **Subjects are public and documented.** Every episode is about something with a verifiable public record — a **public figure**, a **historical case or event**, a **company or practice**, or a **documented collective phenomenon** (Tulipmania, the 1518 dancing plague, the 1835 Moon hoax). For the **Ensayo** track only, this extends to a **publicly released creative work and its documented public reception** (`brain/20` §2), treated as an object of criticism or cultural history — never as footage borrowed to dress an unrelated story. Never private individuals; never a case with no public record. This is the channel's value proposition, not an administrative rule. *(The Ensayo extension was added under change control alongside `brain/20`; the founders' `brain/20` §8 sign-off is what activates the Ensayo track — until then `ideas/tracks.md` marks it unavailable.)*
2. **No episode subject drawn from people Usuario 001 or Usuario 002 personally know** — not even anonymized. Privacy, consent, and conflict of interest. The founders' own migration story is *voice and motive* (About page, the reflection), never an episode subject.
3. **No factual claim without a cited source.** No invented data or quotes, ever.
4. The channel stays **independent in brand, folder, and workflow** — no co-branding, cross-posting, or shared identity with any other channel or venture. *(This is about identity, not money: a disclosed sponsor or a Conquest-owned product CTA is allowed under [02-content-format.md](02-content-format.md) §4 as long as the sponsor has zero say over subject, angle, or script — see [05-independence-and-coi.md](05-independence-and-coi.md).)*

## Success criteria

- Consistent publishing rhythm sustained without burning the non-negotiables.
- Every published episode survives an external fact-check with zero uncited claims.
- Returning audience — retention and subscribers who come back for the next one, measured, not assumed.

## Change control

This charter and the other `brain/` files are **standing rules**. Changing a non-negotiable is a deliberate decision recorded in the git history of this file.
