# 04 — Legal & Ethics Checklist

Not legal advice. This is the working standard; escalate anything uncertain to a lawyer before publishing.

## 1. Subject eligibility

- [ ] Subject is a **public figure**, a **historical case**, or a **company/practice** — not a private individual.
- [ ] There is a **public documentary record**.
- [ ] Passes the independence/COI gate ([brain/05](05-independence-and-coi.md)): no one the founders know, no private tip, tellable from public documentation. (Non-negotiable.)
- [ ] Active proceedings checked. Not a bar to covering the story — court filings are Tier A primary sources — but every disputed claim is attributed ("según la demanda de X", "Y respondió que…"), never asserted as settled fact while it's still contested.

## 2. Defamation / reputation

Higher care for **living people** and **recent events**.
- [ ] Every negative factual claim about a named living person is Tier A/B sourced and attributed on screen.
- [ ] Opinion/interpretation is clearly framed as such ("una lectura posible…").
- [ ] Allegations are labeled as allegations; outcomes (acquittal, settlement, dismissal) are stated.
- [ ] No implication beyond what the record supports (tone, music, and editing can defame — watch the implied claim).
- [ ] Right of reply considered where the person is alive and the claims are serious (note the outreach or why not, in the fact-check sheet).

## 3. Deceased persons / historical figures

- Defamation risk drops but **accuracy and dignity standard stays**. No gratuitous sensationalism, no fabricated inner monologue presented as fact.

## 4. Archival material & copyright

- [ ] Every image/clip/audio logged in `source-log.csv` with rights status: public domain / CC (which license) / licensed / fair-use-quotation / permission obtained.
- [ ] Fair-use-style quotation kept minimal, transformative, attributed. Prefer public domain and licensed.
- [ ] No song lyrics reproduced. Music is licensed library only (log the license).
- [ ] Photos of identifiable private people avoided unless clearly newsworthy and lawful.

## 5. Text & quotation limits (channel-wide)

- No verbatim reproduction of long passages from copyrighted books/articles. Short attributed quotes only; summarize in our own words.
- Scripts must not be reconstructions of a single copyrighted work — synthesize multiple sources.

## 6. Minors

- Extra caution with any identifiable minor, historical or present. Default: do not name or show.

## 7. Sensitive topics

- Violence, suicide, abuse, addiction, illness: handle soberly, no graphic gratuitousness, include help-resource note in description where relevant. Avoid method detail for self-harm.

## 8. Disclosure & honesty

- [ ] Reenactments / recreations / AI-generated / colorized visuals labeled on screen, every appearance. **Recreation & AI illustration follow [15-ai-illustration-protocol.md](15-ai-illustration-protocol.md):** per-episode style (photoreal allowed), where real material is missing / too scarce / rights-locked — may carry most of a material-poor episode — **never a photoreal face of an identifiable real person**, never a fabricated document/record/photograph.
- [ ] Speculation labeled.
- [ ] Description always carries the "Fuentes principales" list.
- [ ] Any sponsorship or paid/product element **verbally disclosed** ("esto lo hace posible…" / "publicidad") **and** on-screen labeled (rótulo «Publicidad» or «Producto propio») the moment it starts. Rules for what's allowed at all: [02-content-format.md](02-content-format.md) §4, [05-independence-and-coi.md](05-independence-and-coi.md) §"Sponsorship line".

## 9. Independence & conflict of interest

- [ ] Independence/COI gate clear ([05-independence-and-coi.md](05-independence-and-coi.md))  — the L2 pass flags obvious risks; the full tick is at Stage 11.
- [ ] No third-party link or pitch in the video. Soft channel CTA only.
